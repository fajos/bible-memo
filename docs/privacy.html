Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: November 19, 2026

Selah Scripture Memory is built with a strict privacy-first and offline-first philosophy. We believe your personal prayer, spiritual meditation, and scripture memorization habits are private and belong solely to you.

1. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information:

  • No Personal Info: We never ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or financial details.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or link any third-party accounts (Apple, Google, Facebook, etc.) to use any feature of the App.
  • No Tracking or Advertising: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, cookies, behavioral profiling, or advertising identifier frameworks (such as Apple's IDFA or Google's AAID).

2. On-Device Local Storage

All your data stays strictly on your physical device inside isolated storage:

  • What is stored locally: Your custom decks, memorized verses, review history (SM-2 repetitions and intervals), quiz scores, study streaks, personal notes, and verse tags.
  • Storage Technology: Data is stored in a local SQLite database (on iOS and Android) or local browser storage (on Web). It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.
  • Full User Ownership: You can export an unencrypted JSON backup of your database anytime via Settings > Backup & Export, create local safety snapshots, or delete any deck, verse, or note at will.

3. Device Permissions & Capabilities

The App requests minimal permissions solely to enable user-chosen features:

Feature Permission / Capability Purpose
Daily Reminders Local Notifications (expo-notifications) Delivers scheduled reminders at your chosen hour. Reminders are scheduled strictly on your local device; no remote push servers or device push tokens are used.
Audio Narration Speech Synthesis (expo-speech) Reads scripture aloud using your device’s on-device text-to-speech engine. No speech or audio is recorded or uploaded.
Tactile Typing Haptics (expo-haptics) Provides subtle vibration feedback for typing recall and quiz responses.
Card Sharing & Backups File Sharing (expo-sharing) Allows you to share styled verse cards or save exported JSON backup files via your system's native share sheet.

4. Network Activity

The App is fully functional without internet connectivity. When an active connection is available, network communication is limited strictly to:

  • Over-The-Air (OTA) Updates: The App queries Expo Application Services (EAS Update) solely to download verified software bug fixes and scripture enhancements. No personal identifiers or user telemetry are transmitted during update checks.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because Selah Scripture Memory collects zero personal information, displays zero advertisements, and contains zero in-app purchases, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

6. Open Source Transparency

Selah Scripture Memory is open source. You are welcome to inspect our code and verify our privacy practices at:

https://github.com/fajos/bible-memo

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted to the repository and accessible directly within the App under Settings > Privacy Policy.
