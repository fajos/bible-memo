Dear Apple App Review Team,

Thank you for your feedback regarding Guideline 2.1 (Information Needed). Below is all requested information regarding Selah Scripture Memory, along with confirmation of the attached demonstration video:

A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS version has been uploaded to the Review Attachments section (or linked below). It demonstrates launching the app from the home screen, browsing starter decks, performing spaced repetition reviews, practicing in interactive Learn modes (Word Typer, Scramble, Audio), and taking Bible quizzes.
- Account Registration / Login / Deletion: Not applicable. The app does not require or support account creation. All features are immediately accessible without sign-in.
- User-Generated Content (UGC): Not applicable. There are no social feeds, public forums, or sharing of user content between users. All decks, verses, notes, and tags are private and stored solely on the user's physical device.
- Paid Content / In-App Purchases: Not applicable. The app is 100% free with no in-app purchases, paid tiers, or subscriptions.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
- Purpose: Selah Scripture Memory is a privacy-first, 100% offline educational and spiritual utility designed to help users memorize, meditate upon, and retain the Holy Bible over a lifetime.
- Target Audience: Christians, students, educators, and anyone wishing to systematically memorize scripture.
- Problem Solved: Memorizing scripture is difficult without structured review intervals. Selah automates the scientifically proven SuperMemo SM-2 Spaced Repetition algorithm, calculating the optimal review time for each verse right before it is forgotten, and reinforces retention through multi-sensory exercises (typing recall, word scramble, fill-in-blanks, audio).
- Value Provided: Long-term memory retention, peaceful distraction-free study, offline accessibility, and zero data tracking.

3. INSTRUCTIONS FOR SETTING UP & ACCESSING MAIN FEATURES
No login credentials or sample files are required. The app launches immediately with pre-loaded starter decks. To review the core flows:
a) Home / Review: Tap "Start Daily Review" or any starter verse. Read the reference, flip the card to see the verse, and select a difficulty rating (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) to witness the SM-2 interval schedule.
b) Library & Decks: Browse pre-loaded decks (Peace & Anxiety, Faith & Trust, Salvation & Grace) or tap "+" to access the complete 66-book Bible verse selector and create a custom deck.
c) Learn Mode: Open any verse and tap "Learn" to explore interactive memory modes:
   - Read & Listen: Offline speech narration with adjustable speed and repeat loops.
   - Word Typer: Active recall where the user types directly into inline blank spaces.
   - Word Scramble: Reordering scrambled words in biblical sequence.
   - Selah Meditation: Audio meditation timer for quiet reflection.
d) Quizzes: Go to the Quiz tab to test verse recognition or play Bible trivia.
e) Settings: Toggle between KJV and WEB public domain translations, customize font size/theme, test offline JSON backups, and view the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS, OR PLATFORMS
The core functionality of the app is completely self-contained and operates 100% offline:
- Data Storage: Local on-device SQLite database (expo-sqlite). No external servers, user databases, or cloud accounts are used.
- Authentication: None.
- Payment Services: None.
- Audio Synthesis: Device-native iOS Text-to-Speech (AVSpeechSynthesizer via expo-speech). No audio is transmitted.
- Notifications: Local on-device scheduling (expo-notifications). No remote push servers or device push tokens are used.
- Software Updates: Expo Application Services (EAS Update) is used solely to download code patches and verified bug fixes. No personal data is collected or transmitted during update checks.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions 100% identically and consistently across all regions worldwide. There are no geoblocked features, regional restrictions, or region-specific content.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY & THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app does not operate in a regulated industry. All scripture texts included in the application are strictly in the public domain:
- King James Version (KJV): Public domain worldwide.
- World English Bible (WEB): Dedicated to the public domain by Rainbow Missions, Inc.
No copyrighted or proprietary third-party materials are used.
