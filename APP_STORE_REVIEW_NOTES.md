3. INSTRUCTIONS FOR SETTING UP & ACCESSING MAIN FEATURES
No credentials or sample files needed. The app launches with pre-loaded starter decks:

b) Library: Browse pre-loaded decks (Peace, Faith, Salvation) or tap "+" to use the 66-book Bible selector to add custom verses.
c) Learn Mode: Open any verse and tap "Learn" to test:
   - Read & Listen: Offline speech narration with adjustable speed.
   - Word Typer: Word-by-word active recall directly into blank spaces.
   - Word Scramble: Reordering words into correct biblical sequence.
   - Selah Meditation: Timed audio meditation.
d) Quizzes: Open the Quiz tab to take verse recall quizzes or Bible trivia.
e) Settings: Switch KJV/WEB translations, adjust font/theme, test offline JSON backups, and view Privacy Policy & Terms.
